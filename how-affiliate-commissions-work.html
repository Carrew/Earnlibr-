How affiliate commissions work

A commission is a reward a promoter may earn when a referred customer completes an eligible action, usually a confirmed sale. Understanding the rules matters more than chasing the biggest percentage.

Fixed versus percentage commissions

A fixed commission pays a stated amount for each qualifying sale. For example, if an offer pays L$200 per confirmed sale, two qualifying sales would mean L$400 in commission. A percentage commission is calculated from the eligible sale value. A 10% commission on an L$1,000 sale is L$100. These examples are illustrations, not EarnLibr earning promises.

What makes a sale eligible?

Read the offer terms before sharing it. Confirm what counts as a completed sale, whether returns or cancellations affect rewards, and who confirms the result. A click alone does not necessarily earn commission.

Understand tracking and payout

A referral link helps connect activity to a promoter. Keep the correct link intact when sharing it. Check your dashboard for the status of referrals and commissions, and review the current payout requirements before planning around expected earnings.

Questions to ask before promoting

What exactly is being sold? What is the price? Is the commission fixed or percentage-based? What must happen before the commission is approved? Is the provider able to fulfill orders? If any detail is unclear, ask before advertising.

Using EarnLibr responsibly

Browse available offers, compare their terms, and promote only products you understand. Be clear that you may earn a commission. Never promise guaranteed income or claim a product does something the provider has not established.
