How to spot online earning scams

An opportunity can look professional and still be misleading. Before sharing personal information, paying a fee, or promoting an offer, check who is behind it and what you are actually expected to do.

Be cautious of guaranteed earnings

Legitimate income depends on real work, sales, or other clearly explained conditions. Claims that everyone will earn a large amount quickly, without effort or risk, deserve extra scrutiny.

Question unexpected fees

Be especially careful when a supposed employer demands money to release wages, unlock tasks, or withdraw earnings. Ask for a written explanation and verify the organization through independent channels.

Protect your accounts

Do not share passwords, one-time verification codes, or account recovery details. Avoid downloading unknown files or signing in through suspicious links. Check the website address carefully.

Verify the business and the task

Look for consistent contact information, clear terms, a real product or service, and a way to ask questions. A social media page, logo, or screenshot alone does not establish legitimacy.

Know the difference between sales and recruitment

A commission tied to an actual qualifying product sale is different from a scheme that primarily rewards recruiting people who must pay to join. Understand where the money comes from.

What to do if something feels wrong

Stop engaging, preserve relevant messages, and use the platform's reporting tools. If money or account access is involved, contact the payment provider or service directly through its official channels.
