The process

Simple steps. Clear expectations.

Understand the process before you create or promote an offer.

01

Businesses submit offers

Providers describe what they sell, their price, available quantity, and proposed commission terms.

02

Offers are reviewed

EarnLibr's review process checks submitted offers before they become available for promotion. Approval does not guarantee a product's results.

03

Promoters share and follow up

Promoters share offer links. Eligible sales or actions are confirmed according to the offer's rules before any commission becomes payable.

Frequently asked questions

Questions worth asking.

What is EarnLibr?

EarnLibr is a platform connecting businesses that create promotional offers with independent promoters who share those offers with potential customers.

Do I earn money just by joining?

No. Joining does not guarantee income. Commissions depend on the specific offer, qualifying results, and the applicable confirmation and payment terms.

Can my business list a product or service?

Providers can submit offers with product information and proposed commission terms. Offers are reviewed before being made available to promoters.

How are commissions calculated?

Offers may use fixed amounts or percentages. The commission value and eligibility requirements depend on the individual campaign. Read the commission guide.

How can I contact EarnLibr?

Reach the team at team.earnlibr@gmail.com or use the contact links below.
